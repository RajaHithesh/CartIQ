Data preparation

From raw orders to model-ready data

Every raw column is cleaned, scaled and encoded by scripts in the src folder before any modelling begins.

🩹

Missing Value Handling

Deletion, mean, median and model-based imputation plus missing indicators — days_since_prior_order is the only column with nulls.

⚖️

Feature Scaling

Standardization (Z-score), Min-Max scaling and L2 normalization bring every numeric feature onto a fair footing.

🔤

Categorical Encoding

Label, One-Hot, Ordinal, Target and embedding-style encoding for aisle, department and eval_set.

✅

Final Dataset

All steps converge into one fully numeric file, ready for machine learning.
